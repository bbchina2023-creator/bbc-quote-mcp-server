### DEV-016.51 multi-item customs freight allocation

A follow-up deterministic calculation audit exposed the next defect: the customs formula used deal-level international freight directly for a one-item deal, but for a multi-item deal it searched only expense rows whose `item_id` already matched the current item. The source freight `1503 USD` is a deal-level expense, so after structured writeback the two items could lose international freight from their customs bases even though the automatic allocation layer had already distributed that expense by the approved `GOODS_VALUE_SHARE` method.

`4.1.51` closes this inconsistency:

1. Multi-item customs duty/VAT now consumes the same confirmed automatic allocation rows that feed item economics.
2. Deal-level international freight is allocated to the two current items by `GOODS_VALUE_SHARE` before customs duty/VAT are calculated.
3. The sum of item customs bases therefore reconciles to the approved current-deal customs base `705821.50 RUB`.
4. Internal duty reconciles to `105873.225 RUB` (`8469.858 CNY`).
5. Internal import VAT reconciles to `178572.8395 RUB` (`14285.82716 CNY`).
6. The complete deterministic current-deal calculation after structured two-item writeback reproduces the verified final quote total `94333.70786 CNY / 1179171.34825 RUB`.
7. The calculation uses current-deal source values for freight (`1503 USD`, USD/RUB `83`, CNY/RUB `12.5`) and existing approved allocation rules; no new commercial rate or business input is invented.
